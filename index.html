PixPort

Free image converter — WebP, AVIF, JPG, PNG, BMP, GIF, ICO · 100% local, nothing uploaded

🔒 Privacy-first · No tracking · No ads

PixPort is a browser extension that converts images between formats entirely on your device. Convert a single image with one right-click, or batch-convert with automatic ZIP packaging.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 2026 · Applies to the PixPort browser extension.

Short version

We collect nothing. We upload nothing. We track no one.

  • All image conversions run locally in your browser — your images never leave your device.
  • The extension contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking scripts.
  • We do not collect, store, or share any personal data, browsing history, or image content.
  • The only network requests the extension makes are: license key verification when you activate Pro (via our proxy server and Creem.io), and opening the upgrade checkout page when you click "Upgrade".

Details

1. Image files. Images you convert are processed in your browser's memory using standard browser APIs. They are never transmitted to us or to any third party. When you close the extension, nothing remains on our side — because there was never anything on our side.

2. Local storage. Your preferences (output format, quality, save location, interface language) and, if you purchase Pro, your license key are stored locally in your browser using chrome.storage. This data never leaves your device and you can erase it anytime by removing the extension.

3. License activation (Pro only). When you activate a Pro license, the license key you enter is sent — through our proxy server — to our payment processor Creem.io for verification. Creem acts as the Merchant of Record: their privacy policy governs the payment and license data they process. We never receive your card details.

4. What we can see. Nothing about your usage. We do not count conversions, do not fingerprint devices, and do not use cookies.

5. Contact. Questions about this policy: wgy117385@gmail.com

How to activate PixPort Pro

  1. Complete your purchase — the license key appears on the payment success page and in your email receipt.
  2. Open the PixPort extension → click the ⚙ Settings icon.
  3. Paste the license key → click Activate. All Pro features unlock instantly, on up to 5 devices.

Didn't receive your license key?

  • The key was displayed on the payment success page — if that tab is still open, copy it there.
  • Check your spam/junk folder for an email from Creem.
  • Log into the Creem customer portal with your purchase email to view your key.
  • Still stuck? Email wgy117385@gmail.com with your payment receipt and we'll resend your key manually.
