FAQ常见问题

Common questions常见问题

What is WebP, and why can't I open it?

WebP is a space-saving image format used by many websites. Some apps, messengers and older tools can't open it — PixPort converts it to universally-supported JPG or PNG instantly.

Are my images uploaded anywhere?

No. Every conversion runs locally inside your browser using standard browser APIs. There are no servers on our side receiving your images — we couldn't see them even if we wanted to.

Is it really free?

Yes — converting single images is free forever, with no ads and no watermarks. The one-time Pro upgrade adds unlimited batch conversion with ZIP packaging and size controls.

Which format should I choose?

Not sure? Pick JPG — it opens everywhere. Choose PNG when you need transparency. WebP for smaller web-ready files.

Where are converted files saved?

To your browser's Downloads folder by default. You can also set it to ask every time, or save into a dedicated PixPort folder — in ? Settings.

什么是 WebP，为什么打不开？

WebP 是很多网站用来节省流量的图片格式，部分应用、聊天工具和旧软件打不开它——PixPort 一键把它转成通用的 JPG 或 PNG。

我的图片会被上传吗？

不会。所有转换都在你的浏览器内用标准浏览器接口完成。我们这边没有任何服务器接收你的图片——想看也看不到。

真的免费吗？

是——单张图片转换永久免费，无广告无水印。一次性 Pro 升级解锁不限量批量转换、ZIP 打包和尺寸控制。

该选哪个格式？

不确定就选 JPG——哪里都能打开。需要透明背景选 PNG，想要更小的网页文件选 WebP。

转换后的文件存到哪了？

默认是浏览器的下载文件夹。也可以在 ? 设置里改成每次询问，或存到专门的 PixPort 文件夹。
